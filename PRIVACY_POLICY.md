# Privacy Policy for MuPlash

**Last updated:** October 29, 2026

MuPlash ("the App", "we", "our") is developed as an interactive music visualizer and ripple simulation application. We respect your privacy and are strictly committed to safeguarding user data. This Privacy Policy outlines our standards and practices.

---

## 1. Information Collection and Storage
MuPlash operates under a strict privacy-first principle:
* **No Personal Data Collected**: We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personally identifiable information (PII).
* **No Account Required**: The App does not require account creation, login, email addresses, or personal profiles.
* **100% On-Device Processing**: All audio playback, shader rendering, fluid physics simulations, and frequency analysis are performed entirely on your device in real time.
* **No External Servers**: We do not operate external servers or collect telemetry, usage metrics, or logs.

---

## 2. Permissions and Audio Handling
* **Local Audio Files (Document Picker / Files App)**: When you choose to play your own audio files, the App accesses them strictly locally on your device solely for playback and real-time visualization during your active session. Files are never uploaded, shared, or indexed.
* **Apple Music / Media Library**: If granted, this allows the App to access DRM-free tracks downloaded to your device library.
* **No Microphone or Audio Recording**: Audio analysis is performed strictly on digital audio stream buffers in memory. The App **never** accesses the device microphone and **never** records or transmits any audio.
* **No Sensitive Permissions**: The App does not request access to Camera, Contacts, Photos, Location, or Health data.

---

## 3. In-App Purchases and Payment Processing
In-App Purchases (such as the MuPlash Lifetime Pass with a 7-Day Free Trial) are processed directly by Apple through **StoreKit 2** and the App Store.
* The developer does not receive, process, or store credit card numbers, billing addresses, or payment credentials.
* All financial transactions are securely managed by Apple and subject to Apple's Privacy Policy and App Store Terms of Service.

---

## 4. Third-Party Analytics and Advertising
* MuPlash contains no third-party advertising SDKs, tracking libraries, or analytics platforms.
* Your activity within the App is not tracked across third-party websites, apps, or services.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy
Because the App does not collect any personal information from any user, it fully complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international child privacy regulations.
