# Privacy Policy for MuPlash

**Last updated:** October 29, 2026

MuPlash ("the App", "we", "our") is developed as an interactive music visualizer and ripple simulation application. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data and permissions.

---

## 1. Information Collection and Storage
MuPlash operates with a strict privacy-first principle:
* **No Personal Data Collected**: We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personally identifiable information (PII).
* **No Account Required**: The App does not require registration, login, email addresses, or user profiles.
* **100% Local Processing**: All sound processing, fluid simulation, shader rendering, and frequency analysis are computed entirely on your device in real time.
* **No External Servers**: We do not maintain external servers to collect user behavior, logs, or telemetry.

---

## 2. Permissions and Audio Handling
* **Local Audio Files (Document Picker / Files App)**: When you choose to play your own audio files, the App accesses them strictly locally on your device for playback and real-time visualization during your active session. Files are never uploaded or shared.
* **Apple Music / Media Library**: If granted, this allows the App to access DRM-free tracks downloaded to your device library.
* **No Microphone or Audio Recording**: Audio analysis is performed strictly on digital playback data in memory. The App **never** accesses the device microphone and **never** records or transmits any audio.
* **No Sensitive Permissions**: The App does not request access to Camera, Contacts, Photos, Location, or Health data.

---

## 3. In-App Purchases and Payment Processing
In-App Purchases (such as the MuPlash Lifetime Pass with 7-Day Free Trial) are processed directly by Apple through **StoreKit 2** and the App Store.
* The developer does not receive, process, or store credit card numbers, billing addresses, or payment credentials.
* All financial transactions are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy and App Store Terms of Service.

---

## 4. Third-Party Analytics and Advertising
* MuPlash does not include third-party advertising SDKs, tracking libraries, or analytics platforms.
* Your activity within the App is not tracked across third-party websites or services.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy
Because the App does not collect any personal information from any user, it fully complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international child privacy regulations.

If this Privacy Policy is updated, the revised version will be published here with an updated revision date.
