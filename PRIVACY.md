# Privacy Policy for MuPlash

**Last updated:** October 28, 2026

MuPlash ("the App") is developed and maintained by the developer. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

## 1. Information Collection and Use
MuPlash does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.

- **No Personal Data Collected**: The App does not require registration, login, or personal identifiers.
- **Local Processing Only**: All sound generation, touch interactions, fluid simulations, and audio effects are computed and rendered 100% locally on your device.
- **No Third-Party Analytics**: The App does not contain third-party tracking libraries, analytics SDKs, or advertising networks.

## 2. Audio and Device Permissions
- **Audio Output**: The App utilizes device audio output for sound synthesis. No microphone recording or external audio transmission is performed.
- **Haptics**: The App utilizes the Taptic Engine for touch feedback locally on the device.

## 3. Children's Privacy
Because the App does not collect any personal data, it complies with regulations regarding children's online privacy (including COPPA).

## 4. Changes to This Privacy Policy
If this Privacy Policy is updated, the revised version will be posted here with an updated revision date.
